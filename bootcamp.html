What's Next: Summer Preparation

Building skills for the future of AI innovation

This summer, AI Coding Academy is launching a focused track to help students prepare for admission into some of the most prestigious AI programs and competitions for middle school students. These opportunities are designed for young innovators eager to explore artificial intelligence in depth, build real-world projects, and connect with like-minded peers and mentors.

  • Hands-on AI projects using Python, computer vision, and machine learning
  • Guidance in building a personal portfolio of work
  • Training in how to present and communicate technical ideas effectively
  • Step-by-step support with application requirements and submissions

Featured Programs

🧠 MIT Hackathon

A global virtual event organized by MIT RAISE, where students design and build AI-powered solutions that address real-world challenges aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

The next hackathon will take place in 2026, and summer 2025 is the perfect time to begin skill-building and portfolio development.

raise.mit.edu/events/global-ai-hackathon-2025

🛠 ODE Design Hackathon

Hosted by Duke University, this engineering-focused hackathon introduces middle school students to rapid prototyping, programming, and design thinking. Participants build creative tech solutions with a focus on innovation and real-world impact.

sites.duke.edu/odeatduke/hackathon-for-middle-school-students

🤖 Stanford AI4ALL

A highly selective summer program led by researchers at Stanford University, focused on human-centered artificial intelligence, ethical innovation, and inclusion in tech. Students engage in hands-on research, mentorship, and collaborative AI projects.

ai-4-all.org/stanford
