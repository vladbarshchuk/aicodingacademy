Meet Our Team

Our Mission

Empowering the next generation of AI innovators

At AI Coding Academy, we believe that early exposure to artificial intelligence and coding creates pathways to future innovation. Our mission is to make advanced technology education accessible, engaging, and inspiring for middle school students.

Founded by a team of educators and industry professionals, our academy bridges the gap between traditional education and cutting-edge technology. We focus on hands-on learning, creative problem-solving, and ethical considerations in AI development.

Through our specialized curriculum, students not only learn to code but also develop critical thinking skills, collaborate effectively, and build confidence in their ability to shape the technological future.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our educational approach

Hands-On Learning

We believe in learning by doing. Our curriculum emphasizes practical projects and real-world applications over theoretical concepts alone.

Ethical Innovation

We teach students to consider the ethical implications of AI development, fostering responsible innovation from the beginning.

Inclusive Excellence

We're committed to making AI education accessible to students from all backgrounds, creating pathways for diverse voices in technology.

Growth Mindset

We encourage students to embrace challenges, persist through obstacles, and understand that abilities can be developed through dedication and hard work.
